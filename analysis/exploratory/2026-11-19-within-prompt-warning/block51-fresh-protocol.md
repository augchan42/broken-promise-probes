# Test the fixed block 51 predictor on fresh order replays

20 November 2026. This protocol was written before generating the new replays. The earlier 87.5% result selected block 51 on four mixed positions. The scripted test showed a response to a written choice. Here Qwen writes its own reasoning and orders.

- Use one previously unused eligible saved order prompt per evaluation game. Exclude the 12 prompts used in the within-position sweep and the four in the scripted test. Within each game, prefer prompts whose archived orders broke the promise, then break ties by a fixed hash of the order call ID. This enriches for positions likely to give both outcomes. It does not estimate the breach rate across all positions.
- Use four new samples per prompt with separate, fixed seeds for reasoning and answers. Give Qwen the exact original order prompt, checkpoint, sampling settings and budgets. No scripted choice or continuation goes into its context. Apply the original order legality and promise rules. A reasoning-limit closure contains the final generated token only once. Saved original orders select cases and verify the scoring rules. They do not enter the evaluation.
- At exactly 270 generated reasoning tokens, use the same local 9B reader, prompt, score and context, and the same 27B block 51 readout and fit as in the sweep. Reproduce the original block 51 result from its development fit and save the fitted coefficients before generation. Score text and the fixed combination on the same prefixes. A replay ending before token 270 or giving invalid orders contributes no pair. Repeat after excluding forced reasoning closures.
- Report the number of valid draws and mixed positions, the text and combined ranking percentages, score differences per position, and the range after leaving one position out. Count a tie as half and give each mixed position equal weight. Use at most 32 new draws and report any shortage of mixed positions. A consistent early gain here requires at least four mixed positions, a positive mean after dropping each position in turn, and a positive uncapped mean. With fewer mixed positions, report the observations as a small follow-up.

I expect a smaller gain than the 31-point increase in the sweep. I am uncertain that it will survive leaving out one position. Block 51 responded when a short script chose a covered move, and the effect weakened when earlier reasoning was restored. A positive gain across fresh positions would update me toward a useful early warning during order reasoning. A zero or negative gain would suggest the earlier numbers were sensitive to the positions or the choice of block.
