# TARA snapshots — 15 November 2026

Downloaded copies of project and course materials, not screenshots or link-only placeholders. Original source links remain in the [source index](../../README.md) and in [manifest.json](manifest.json).

The Hong Kong career-planning deck is a downloaded original PowerPoint file, not a native Google Slides document.

## Integrity and changes

- PDF exports were downloaded from Google Drive; reading/session PDFs came from their Slack attachments.
- The participant guide is a **redacted copy**: its teacher/staff contact directory on page 18 was removed, including email links. Other content is retained.
- Notebook cell source is unchanged. Saved outputs, execution counts and transient Colab/widget metadata were cleared. **No notebook was executed.**
- The manifest records original and archived SHA-256 hashes, byte sizes, source IDs/URLs and transformations. `SHA256SUMS` checks archived files.
- Personal directories, the mentor/contact spreadsheet, private DMs and private feedback are excluded.
- Third-party materials remain attributed to their original authors and retain their own terms. The repository's MIT licence does not relicense these materials.
